How it works

  1. Press Start SOS and allow your location when the browser asks.
  2. You get a private link that shows your live location. Only your first name is shown.
  3. Send the link to your contacts with the buttons that appear. SafeRadar AI cannot send SMS or WhatsApp messages by itself: nothing reaches anyone until you press send in your own messaging app.
  4. The link stops showing your location when you stop sharing, and after {{ "%g"|format(duration_hours) }} hours at the latest.

Keep this page open while you share: phones usually stop sending location when the page is in the background.
