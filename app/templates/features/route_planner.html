Please read

When you press Find routes, your chosen start and end points are sent from this server to an external routing service (OSRM) so it can calculate the routes. SafeRadar AI does not save them.

The risk index is a model estimate that compares places and times within the data the model learned from, so a low index does not mean safe. Parts of a route with no estimate are unknown, and unknown does not mean safe. Use your own judgement and, in an emergency, call 112.
